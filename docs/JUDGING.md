# How projects are judged

From the organizers' slide (shown Fri Nov 20, 10:26 AM). Submit by 3:00 PM. Build on Akamai Cloud to qualify for prizes (Akamai Cloud credits: $1,000 / $500 / $250).

| Weight | Criterion | What judges want shown |
|--------|-----------|------------------------|
| 40% | Other Akamai Cloud services | A feature that depends on the services you chose |
| 20% | Akamai Cloud Inference | The model's task and how you checked its output |
| 20% | Usefulness | Who needs the app and how it helps them |
| 20% | Creativity | What you tried and what makes it different |
